Which passwords work here?

GB2 Training (9 symbols), Thank-You (12), SOS (13), and Revival (15). The same mapping works with both the classic and shadowed English fonts.

A checksum check catches many copying errors. It cannot confirm that a dungeon is unlocked, that a rescue is still available, or that a Revival or Thank-You password belongs to your rescue.

Big Moai’s four-character gift codes and the six-character Clear Campaign / True Wanderer certificates are separate systems. Passwords from other Shiren games are not supported.
